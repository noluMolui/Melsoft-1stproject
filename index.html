Coffee, books and chill

Welcome to NoluLoves, a warm, intentional cafe where every cup is made with care and every corner invites calm. We blend specialty coffee, homemade pastries, and a quiet, welcoming atmosphere so students, creators, and neighbours can find focus and company.

Our space is designed to be used slowly: bring a book, open your laptop, or meet a friend for a soft conversation. We keep noise low, music gentle, and service friendly so you can feel at ease while you work or unwind.

See menu

Fine teas & cold drinks

We serve a wonderful selection of loose-leaf teas, comforting herbal blends, and refreshing cold drinks made fresh every day. Whether you want a hot, soothing cup to warm up or a chilled, fruity iced drink, we have something perfect for your mood.

Quiet space

The room is calm so you can study, read, or do your work comfortably. We offer plenty of power outlets for your laptops, reliable high-speed Wi-Fi, and comfortable seating designed for long focus sessions or quiet thinking.

Books too!!

There are books for reading in the cafe, and some books you can buy. Our curated shelves feature a mix of classic literature, modern fiction, and inspiring design art books. Feel free to grab a novel to flip through while you sip your drink.

Why Noluloves?

We made a space that is peaceful but also friendly. It is meant for people who want beautiful refreshments and a quiet place to focus. Whether you are typing out a university essay, meeting up with a colleague to brainstorm, or just unwinding with a great book after a busy day, we provide the perfect cozy backdrop for your routine.

We believe that a great cafe is more than just a place that sells drinks; it is a small sanctuary away from the rush of everyday life. That is why we pay close attention to keeping the atmosphere gentle, calm, and welcoming for everyone who steps through our doors.

Beautiful Pastries

Cold Drinks

A place to stay

Here you can stay as long as you want, enjoy a cup, and get work done in a calm environment. We don't rush you out the door. We want you to treat this space like your second home or your ideal remote workspace.

Hours & Visits

We are open Monday through Saturday from 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and Sundays from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Stop by early to catch the morning baking smell, or join us in the quiet afternoons to wrap up your daily tasks in peace.
