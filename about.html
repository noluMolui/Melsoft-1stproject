About our cafe

This page explains who we are and what we want the cafe to feel like.

Our story

We opened Noluloves because we were tired of two extremes: working alone at home, or trying to concentrate in loud, overcrowded coffee shops. We wanted a third space, a place that feels like home, but with the gentle energy of other people around. Our first shop started with a single espresso machine, a few second-hand books, and a clear promise: to make space for focus and kind human moments.

Over the years we have grown carefully: we partner with local bakers, source beans from nearby roasters, and shape the room so it stays peaceful and welcoming.

What we do

Every morning we roast beans chosen for clarity and sweetness, bake simple pastry recipes from scratch, and arrange our seating to offer cozy nooks and long communal tables. We keep fast WiFi but we don’t over-amplify the space, that way people can work, write, or read without being interrupted.

We also host small community events: quiet reading hours, slow-writing sessions, and occasional open-mic evenings in the back room. These events are gentle, they are meant to bring people together without breaking the calm.

Our simple rules

  • Guard the Quiet: Please keep conversations soft and use headphones for video or audio.
  • Use the Call Booths: If you need privacy or a louder conversation, the soundproof booths are for that.
  • Share with Care: Be kind to other guests and the space, treat it like your living room away from home.

These simple habits help everyone get their best work done and enjoy a calm, human cafe experience.
