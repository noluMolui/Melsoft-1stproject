Books & sourcing

Our shelves hold a handpicked selection of fiction, poetry, practical work books, and leisurely reads. We rotate titles often; borrow one to read in the cafe or buy a copy to support our local booksellers.

We source beans from trusted small roasters who share our values about quality and fair treatment. If you have allergies or dietary needs, ask our team, we are happy to suggest alternatives or adapt where possible.
